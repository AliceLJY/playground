# Third-party notices

`sandworm.html` embeds a minified bundle of Three.js 0.160.1, including OrbitControls from its examples. Its MIT licence and copyright notice are kept in a comment at the top of the bundled script and in `THREE-LICENSE.txt`.

Design reference: [AYi's seven prompt rounds and reference images](https://x.com/AYi_AInotes/status/2104826052979597409). The exhibit was implemented independently from those written prompts; none of the reference author's code, model files or screenshots are used. The sandworm subject is inspired by *Dune*; no film assets are used and the design is fictional.

Geometry, terrain, diagrams, interface and code were made for this exhibit. There are no external image, font, audio or model dependencies.
