# Third-party notices

`share.js` bundles Three.js 0.186.1, including OrbitControls and BufferGeometryUtils from its examples. Its MIT licence and copyright notice are preserved in `THREE-LICENSE.txt`.

Content:

- Input floor plan: [Sample Floorplan.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sample_Floorplan.jpg) by Boereck (2006), Wikimedia Commons, public domain. The plan, room and furniture data bundled in `share.js` were read from it. `assets/sun.png` overlays the sunlight heat map on that image; `assets/plan-A01.png` is a drawing generated from the extracted data.
- `assets/render-oak.jpg` and `assets/render-walnut.jpg` are path-traced renders. The furniture and soft furnishings in them are Poly Haven models, and the sky light is the Poly Haven HDRI "Meadow" by Sergej Majboroda, all CC0. Only the rendered images are included; no model or HDRI files are distributed.
- Inspiration: [a floor-plan-to-render video by @AndyL5cc](https://x.com/AndyL5cc/status/2104917432825680278). None of its code or assets are used.

Walls, openings, stairs, cabinetry, procedural textures, the CAD drawing, the sunlight calculation and this page were made for the original project.
