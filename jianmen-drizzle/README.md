# Drizzle at Jianmen

Step into Gao Jianfu's 1926 landscape. The hanging scroll is inscribed with Lu You's poem *Light Rain on the Road to Jianmen Pass* and signed "after the Jiannan poems"; in it a rider on a donkey is heading up the gorge to the pass. When the painting dissolves you are standing where he stands, above the gorge, in fine rain and drifting cloud. Turn your head to find the places the poem names; each time you settle on one, a line is brushed onto the poem slip:

*Dust of the road and stains of wine on my robe; / far travels, and every place has stirred my soul. / Am I a poet after all? / In fine rain, on a donkey, I ride into Jianmen.*

Turn around: Gao Jianfu never painted that side.

**Live: https://aliceljy.github.io/playground/jianmen-drizzle/**

## Controls

| | Keyboard / mouse | Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Turn your head | drag, or arrow keys / WASD | one-finger drag |
| Look closer / wider | wheel | pinch |
| Sound | the round sound button, top left | same |

Hold your gaze on a place for a second; the ink ring in the middle fills. The poem slip, top right, starts with the four lines traced in faint red; each find inks its line. The compass bar along the bottom shows which way each of the four places lies, marked ↑ if it is above you and ↓ if below. After 25 seconds without a find, a short hint and an arrow appear. A visit takes two or three minutes.

## How it was made

- Scene: the painting, with its inscription cropped off, went to [PINOC](https://viggle.ai/pinoc) (Viggle's JST-2 world model), which built a Gaussian-splat scene from that single image; one hole was then filled. It is reconstructed from the painter's viewpoint, so you can turn your head and see behind him, but you cannot walk far: the painting looks down into the gorge from high up, so the camera hangs in the air.
- The seam behind you: the generated panorama's two ends don't line up straight behind the viewer, and filling holes doesn't fix it. A shader turns that seam into a waterfall dropping out of the cloud.
- Everything that moves is code: the shader drifts the bright grey splats (the mist); the drizzle is a layer of fine ink strokes that pans as you turn; the rain, a plucked string for each find and the donkey bell at the end are synthesised live.
- Rendering: PlayCanvas 2.18, with the scene converted to SOG: computers load the full scene (720k splats, 8.3 MB), phones default to a half-density version (360k, 4.4 MB); `?q=full` or `?q=lite` switches by hand.
- Game logic lives in `src/core.js`, independent of rendering and runnable in Node; an autopilot drives the tests and the fixed-view screenshots.

The idea follows @gkxspace's *Walk Into Van Gogh* ([walk-into-van-gogh](https://github.com/yuwen-cool/walk-into-van-gogh), MIT): one painting becomes a scene, and you turn around to see the half the painter never painted. The code here is our own.

## Local

```bash
npm ci --ignore-scripts && npm rebuild esbuild   # playcanvas pulls in a native canvas package it doesn't need; skip building it
node --test tests/*.test.cjs
node tools/build.mjs          # output in dist/; serve dist/index.html from any static server
```

Query options: `?view=hero|gate|back` for fixed views, `?auto` for the autopilot to find all four places and reach the ending, `?demo` for a slow walkthrough from the normal opening (`tools/demo.cjs` records it as a portrait MP4 and logs each event's frame time; `tools/demo-music.py` scores qin-style music to those times); `&still=1` freezes time and rain, `&hud=0` and `&rain=0` remove the interface and the rain, `&seam=off` removes the waterfall and shows the raw seam.

Browser checks: `tools/check.cjs` (pixel readings and frame rate), `tools/layout.cjs` (the enter button fully visible and tappable on short screens) and `tools/first30.cjs` (a recording of the first 30 seconds); usage is at the top of each file.

## Who it's for, AI assets

- Who it's for: People who like Chinese painting, the Lingnan School or this Lu You poem, and people curious how one painting becomes a 3D place you can stand in.
- AI assets: The 3D scene was generated by PINOC (Viggle JST-2) from Gao Jianfu's painting, which is in the public domain; the painting and the poem are not AI-generated; drifting mist, waterfall, drizzle, interface and sound are code; code written through human–AI collaboration.
