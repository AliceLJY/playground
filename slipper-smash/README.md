# Slipper Smash

A rainy night in an old Guangzhou alley, and the cockroaches keep leaping up. Hold and swipe across them to flatten each one with a flip-flop; chained hits multiply the score. Leave the milk tea cups with the red ring alone, because the round ends once three are spilled. Last 45 seconds and the muscle-bound boss walks in: wear it down within 25 seconds and you get a photo together.

**[Play online](https://aliceljy.github.io/playground/slipper-smash/)**

A round takes about a minute. It plays on phones in either orientation and on desktop.

## Controls

- Phone: hold and swipe across a cockroach; a tap also counts.
- Desktop: hold the mouse button and drag. Space or Enter starts a round.
- Sound can be switched off at the bottom left of the title and result screens.

## How it is built

- The one-page spec is [SPEC.md](SPEC.md): colours, composition ranges, rule numbers, performance budget and ten acceptance items.
- The rules live in `src/core.js` with no drawing code, so they run in Node. The tests measure real simulated rounds: where arcs peak, how many things share the screen, the share of milk tea, and round length and win rate for four styles of play.
- Drawing uses a 2D canvas. The flip-flop, the cups and the effects are drawn in code, and all sound is synthesised with WebAudio.
- Fixed views: `?view=hero`, `?view=stress`, `?view=boss`, `?view=win`. The debugging hook is `window.__slipper`.

## Run locally

```bash
cd slipper-smash && python3 -m http.server 8931
```

Open `http://127.0.0.1:8931/`. Tests: `node --test tests/*.test.cjs`. There is no build step; publishing copies `index.html`, `src/` and `assets/` as they are.

## Assets and origin

- Swiping to smash things is the common mechanic of Fruit Ninja style games. This one was prompted by [Pumpkin Smash](https://frank-otto.github.io/pumpkin-smash/), a Halloween browser game; its code was not read and none of its assets are used.
- The cockroach, alley and character images in `assets/` were generated by the author with AI image tools, and the character is fictional. The cockroach sprites are cut from white-background originals by `tools/make-sprites.cjs`.

## Verification

See [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md). Feel, sound and whether it is fun still need a person to play it.

## Who it's for, AI assets

- Who it's for: Anyone who wants a one-minute swipe game on the phone; Guangzhou folks will recognise the slipper and the cockroaches.
- AI assets: The cockroach, alley and character images in `assets/` were generated with an AI image tool (the character is fictional); flip-flop, milk tea, effects and sound are code; code written through human–AI collaboration.
