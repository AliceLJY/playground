# Shadow Gong · Flow sprite

A copy of [Shadow Gong](../shadow-gong/) with the game logic, timing and hit windows untouched; only the hero, Tongluo Zai, is swapped from the code-drawn riveted puppet to a shadow-puppet sprite cut frame by frame from Google Flow image-to-video takes. Play both to compare.

**[Play online](https://aliceljy.github.io/playground/shadow-gong-flow/)** · add `?rig=1` to switch back to the code-drawn hero.

## How it was made

- Master: web ChatGPT drew the hero after the original design as a translucent leather shadow puppet with riveted joints and control rods, on flat magenta.
- Motion: Google Flow (Omni 1.2 Flash, first-frame mode, locked camera), one 8 s take each for idle, walk in place, gong block, finishing blow and hurt; reviewed at 360p, then upscaled to 720p inside Flow (the upscale used no credits).
- Keying and packing: the video2dsprite pipeline (soft magenta key with edge despill) at 12 fps; idle (27 frames) and walk (28 frames) loops chosen by the smallest seam difference, block and finishing blow aligned on their contact frame to the game's hit time, hurt played over the state duration. 125 frames in one 2880×2992 WebP atlas (`assets/`).
- Wiring: `src/flowsprite.js` picks a frame from the existing simulation state only; `src/world.js` draws the hero as one textured plane placed, flipped and rotated from the original rig pose. Sparks and stars still come from the rig's gong point.
- `SPEC.md` and `VERIFICATION.md` are the original's and describe the code-drawn hero; this copy changes only how the hero is drawn.

## Known differences

- Dodge, knock-down, guard break and victory reuse walk, hurt and gong frames.
- No gong-face glow on a perfect parry.
- The sprite's gong is a little smaller and the hero slightly shorter than the code version.

## Who will open it, AI assets

- Who: players of the original who want to see whether AI video works as game animation.
- AI assets: the hero master was generated by web ChatGPT and its five motions by Google Flow image-to-video, then keyed into frames; enemies, stage and sound are the original code-drawn and synthesised ones; code written in human–AI collaboration.
