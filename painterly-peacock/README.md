# Painterly Peacock

A three-dimensional peacock with an oil-paint feel, standing on a warm ivory ground. Move the mouse and it walks after the pointer; rest the pointer on it and it shakes its head while the train opens feather by feather, then folds again when the pointer leaves. Click one of its train feathers and that feather comes loose and drifts to the ground; the bird walks over, lowers its head, picks it up, turns its head and puts it back into the very socket it came from. Scroll down and it steps closer and pecks hard; scroll up and it backs away. Space makes it crouch, jump and land.

On a phone, tap the ground to lead it and tap a feather to pluck it; four buttons at the bottom open the train, step closer, back off and jump. Sound is off by default and switched on top right; every sound is synthesized on the spot with Web Audio.

All shapes, colours, brush grain and sounds are generated by code. No external models, textures, images or audio files. The shape is illustrative and does not depict a particular breed.

## Run and check

```sh
npm ci
node tools/build.mjs            # single file dist/index.html
node --test tests/*.test.cjs    # Node tests: full pickup chain, feather count, beak and quill never apart, channel ownership, safe zone, walking alignment, fan, materials, budget, page wording
node tools/browser-check.cjs    # real Chrome: 10 fixed-state screenshots, layout, pixel readings, real mouse and touch input, page errors
node tools/preview.cjs          # CPU composition preview for machines without WebGL (not a rendered screenshot)
```

Source entry: run `node tools/serve.cjs . 8941` and open `http://127.0.0.1:8941/dev.html`. Fixed states use `?scene=stand` (folded, standing), `display` (full fan), `fallen` (feather on the ground), `carrying` (feather in the beak on its way back), `returned` (after the insert); each is reached by real play from a fixed seed and then paused. `window.__PLAYTEST__` provides `ready`, `snapshot()`, `reset()`, `setSeed(n)`, `step(ms)`, `hover(true/false)`, `walkTo(screenX, screenY)`, `pluck(id)`, `jump()`, `peck()` and `setScene(name)`. See [SPEC.md](SPEC.md) for the acceptance criteria and for what is still unverified. Three.js is pinned at 0.186.1; its MIT licence is kept in the generated page.

## Who it's for, AI assets

- Who it's for: anyone who likes birds and brushwork and wants to play with a peacock for a minute or two on a phone or a computer.
- AI assets: code and documentation were completed through AI collaboration. All geometry, colours, brush grain, animation and sound are procedural. No AI-generated or imported images, models, motion files or audio are used.
