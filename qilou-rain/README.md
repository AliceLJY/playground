# Qilou Rain

A rainy night on an arcade street in old Guangzhou. Three manholes have been pushed open and giant cockroaches are pouring out. Armed with a bug sprayer and one plastic slipper, put the lids back one by one and catch the last ferry.

[Play online](https://aliceljy.github.io/playground/qilou-rain/)

## How to play

- Desktop: `W A S D` to move, mouse to look, left button to spray, right button to slap, hold `E` to seal a manhole, `R` to swap the tank, `Shift` to run, Space to jump, `Esc` to pause. Without a mouse, the right hand uses `J` to spray, `K` to slap, the arrow keys to look and `L` to seal, so the left hand only moves.
- Phone: drag on the left half to move and on the right half to look; spray, slap, swap and jump buttons sit at the bottom right, and a seal button appears next to a manhole.
- Crawlers rear up before biting. Flyers hover, then dive in a straight line. The armoured one shrugs off spray from the front: bait it into a column so it flips over, then hit it.

## How it was made

The street, the arcades, the signs, the rain, the reflections, the cockroaches, the sprayer in your hand and every sound are generated by code when the page opens. There are no image, model or audio files; the lettering on the signs is drawn on a canvas at run time. The look of the cockroach and its tripod gait grouping follow the American cockroach in [gz-roach](../gz-roach/) in this repository. Rendering uses Three.js (MIT), bundled into a single HTML file that works offline.

```
npm ci
node --test tests/*.test.cjs      # headless logic tests
node tools/build.mjs              # bundles dist/index.html
node tools/serve.cjs . 8947       # local preview at http://127.0.0.1:8947/dist/index.html
NODE_PATH="$(npm root -g)" node tools/browser-check.cjs http://127.0.0.1:8947/dist/index.html shots/check
```

The spec, numbers, fixed camera views and acceptance results are in [SPEC.md](SPEC.md) (Chinese).

## Who this is for, and AI material

For anyone curious how far a first-person game can go on code alone, and for anyone who dislikes cockroaches enough to enjoy this.

The code, the rules, the numbers and the tests were written with AI assistance (Claude Code). No AI-generated images, models, animation or audio are in the page. One mock-up image was generated at the start as a colour reference and is not in the repository. Until people have played it, the feel and the difficulty are known only from autopilot runs.
