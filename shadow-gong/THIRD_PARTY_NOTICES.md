# Third-party notices

`vendor/three.min.js` is the Three.js r160 build already used by the other toys in this repository. Its MIT license and copyright notice are preserved in `vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt` and in the generated standalone HTML.

Puppets, scenery, interface, game simulation and synthesised sound were created for this project. There are no external image, font, audio or model dependencies.

Mechanics reference: [Long Wind](https://github.com/jbang2004/long-wind). Its parry design — press timestamps, telegraphs aligned to simulation time, attack tokens, posture and executions, hit-stop and slow motion — was studied as a written analysis and reimplemented from scratch. None of its code or assets (character models, motion capture, textures, sounds or text) are used here.
